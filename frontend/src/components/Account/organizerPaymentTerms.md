## Payments run through Stripe, not KeebMeet

To sell tickets, you connect a Stripe account through KeebMeet. Stripe handles the actual moving of money — verifying who you are, processing payments, and paying you out. You'll agree to Stripe's own terms when you set it up, and those apply to you directly.

We're not a bank or a payment processor. **Your ticket money never passes through us** — it goes straight from your attendees to your Stripe account.

## You're the one selling the tickets

When someone buys a ticket to your meetup, that sale is between **you and them**. You're the seller. KeebMeet just provides the platform that lists your event and runs checkout — we're not part of the sale, and we're not the ones selling anything.

We're independent from each other. This isn't a partnership, and neither of us can make promises on the other's behalf.

Stripe charges its own processing fees on each payment — those come out of your sales.

KeebMeet does not currently charge a platform fee. If we ever do, we'll show it to you when you set up the event, before you publish it. We won't quietly add anything to your prices.

Stripe pays you out to your bank account on their schedule. We don't control when that happens, or any holds Stripe puts on your account. Keep your payout info accurate so nothing gets stuck.
